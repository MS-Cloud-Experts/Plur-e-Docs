# **Introduction**
This document explains the Pick and Package processes, contrasting the procedure in Business Central and the procedure in Plur-e, highlighting the benefits of the mobile application.

# **PICK**
The Pick process in Business Central involves collecting items from the warehouse to fulfill sales, transfer, or production orders. A Warehouse Pick document is generated, detailing the items, quantities, and locations. Staff follow these instructions to collect the items, and upon confirming the pick in the system, the inventory is automatically updated. The collected items are then prepared for shipping, transfer, or use in production.

The Plur-E mobile app is built to simplify warehouse picking operations through seamless integration with Business Central. Using Plur-E, employees can record picks from any location within the warehouse, avoiding the need to go back to a stationary terminal and saving significant time. The application enables direct use of scanners, accelerating workflows and minimizing mistakes in data entry. This mobile solution improves productivity while reducing the likelihood of errors from manual input, offering a quicker, more precise, and efficient experience.

# **PACKAGE**

Plur-E allows in the picking process to create packages to be shipped by shipping agents. This improves the organization and visibility of shipments by enabling automatic labeling and accurate tracking. It optimizes time and reduces errors by linking packages to specific orders, facilitates rate calculation and shipment consolidation to save costs, and streamlines management by integrating with multiple carriers. It also improves the customer experience by providing tracking notifications and faster deliveries, making the process more efficient and reliable.
