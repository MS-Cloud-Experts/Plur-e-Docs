**What is PlurE?**  
PlurE is an innovative Android mobile application that optimizes and manages key business processes such as inventory, sales, and warehousing through integration with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. PlurE enhances operational efficiency by enabling real-time access and control of these processes using advanced AI technologies.

**Key Benefits**  
- **Process Automation**: Efficient management of inventory, sales, and warehousing.  
- **Improved Decision-Making**: Smart predictions powered by AI.  
- **Accessibility Anywhere**: User-friendly mobile application.  
- **Full Integration with Dynamics 365 Business Central.**  

---

### **Key Features**  

#### **Inventory Management**  
The app enables efficient control and management of inventories, ensuring up-to-date and accurate stock level information.

#### **Sales Optimization**  
With its integration into Dynamics 365, PlurE facilitates the entire sales cycle, from order registration to tracking invoices and payments, in an automated and simplified manner.

#### **Warehouse Management**  
Optimizes product distribution within warehouses, improves space utilization, and reduces inventory management costs.

---

### **Installation and Setup**  

#### **System Requirements**  
- **Operating System**: Android 9.0 or higher.  
- **Internet Connection**: Required for integration with Microsoft Dynamics 365.  
- **Storage Space**: At least 100 MB of free space.  

#### **Installation Steps on Android**  
1. Download the app from the Google Play Store.  
2. Open the application and follow the instructions to link it to your Dynamics 365 Business Central account.  
3. Configure your initial preferences.  

#### **Initial Integration Setup**  
- Ensure you have an active Dynamics 365 account.  
- Enter your account credentials to enable data synchronization.

### **Using the Application**  

#### **Navigating the Interface**  
PlurE’s intuitive interface features a navigation panel that allows quick access to key functions, such as inventory management, sales, and warehouse operations.

#### **Inventory Management Features**  
Access product records, update quantities, perform inventory adjustments, and more, all from your Android device.

#### **Sales and Order Management**  
View current orders, create new sales opportunities, update order statuses, and generate sales reports in real-time.

#### **Warehouse and Space Monitoring**  
Track available warehouse space, adjust product distribution, and ensure proper product placement.

PlurE’s AI capabilities enhance decision-making by analyzing large datasets to provide forecasts and suggestions for optimizing operations.

AI also identifies inefficiencies in inventory and sales management, offering recommendations to streamline these processes.
